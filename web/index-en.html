FAQ

Are my files really not uploaded?

Correct. The PDF engine (MuPDF) is packaged inside the application. Everything happens in your computer's memory and on its disk. The app makes one small license check at startup (a few dozen bytes, machine ID only) — no file content. You can block its network access with a firewall and all features still work during the trial.

Which engine is used? Is conversion quality good?

PDF operations use MuPDF and pypdf, both industry-standard open source engines. Office to PDF calls your installed Word/Excel/PowerPoint directly, so output quality is exactly what Office produces — better than most online converters.

Why is it so cheap?

Because there's no server to rent for you. Online tools charge monthly, and a large part of that goes to servers and bandwidth. This app runs on your machine, so that cost doesn't exist.

I changed computers. What now?

Licenses are tied to the machine. Use the trial runs on the new machine first, then contact us to transfer your license.

Does it work with scanned documents?

Merge, split, compress, watermark and encrypt all work with scans. "PDF to Word" and "Redact" need a text layer, so pure image scans require OCR — results for those two are limited on scanned files.

Can I get an invoice?

Yes. Contact us after payment with your billing details.
